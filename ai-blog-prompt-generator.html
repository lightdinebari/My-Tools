Pro Tips for Better Results
Get the most out of every generated prompt
Be specific with your title. "How to Speed Up a Blogger Blog" produces a better prompt than just "Blogger speed".
Include 3 to 5 keywords. One primary keyword plus 2 to 4 secondary ones gives the AI the right SEO context.
Add internal links with descriptions. Telling the AI what each link is about helps it weave them in naturally rather than dumping them at the end.
Review before pasting into Blogger. Always check external links open correctly and verify internal links point to live pages.
Paste into Blogger HTML view directly. The prompt instructs the AI to output Blogger-safe HTML. Switch to HTML mode before pasting, not Compose mode.

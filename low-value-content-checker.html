What Is the Low Value Content Checker?

The Lightrux Low Value Content Checker is a free self-assessment tool that helps bloggers and website owners understand whether their content meets Google's quality standards — before they get rejected by AdSense or penalized in search rankings.

Instead of guessing what is wrong with your site, answer 10 targeted questions based on real Google quality signals and receive an instant content quality score with clear action steps to improve.

What Does Google Consider Low Value Content?

Google's quality systems flag websites and pages that show these signals:

  • Copied, rewritten, or scraped content from other sources
  • Short articles with little depth or useful information
  • Content that lacks personal experience, opinion, or original insight
  • Pages with almost no text or meaningful value for the reader
  • Sites focused mainly on affiliate links or ads rather than helping users
  • Duplicate or near-identical content across multiple pages
  • Missing essential pages such as About, Contact, or Privacy Policy
  • Excessive ads, popups, or intrusive elements that reduce user experience
  • Outdated content that has never been refreshed or updated
